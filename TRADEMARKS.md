common-law rights already apply from first use.

## What's fine, no permission needed

- Referring to Brigade-ITSM by name in your own writing, talks, docs or code.
- Saying your product or integration "integrates with Brigade-ITSM" or "works with
  Brigade-ITSM".

## What needs written permission

- Using "Brigade-ITSM" (or anything confusingly close to it) in the name of your own
  product, service or company.
- Offering any hosted or SaaS service under the Brigade-ITSM name.
- Reseller or partner use of the name or logo.

## Logo

The Brigade-ITSM logo may be used unmodified, and only to refer to the product
itself. It may not be altered, combined with another mark, or used in a way that
implies endorsement.
