# Brigade-ITSM 🛎️

> 🎫 IT service management, built as a set of small services.

**Every order, done right by your team.**

Brigade is an IT service management (ITSM) platform from the rabbit hole tech, llc, following ITIL practice. Your IT team uses it to take in, track and resolve work:

- **Records:** incidents, problems, changes, tasks and requests, each with its own state, owner, SLA timer and history.
- **Major incidents:** a command track with after-action reviews.
- **Change control:** approval chains and a Change Advisory Board.
- **Knowledge base**, a **CMDB** with a service map, and **satisfaction surveys**.
- **Workflow designer** for automations.

The name comes from the kitchen brigade: every role has its station, and every ticket gets called, worked and sent out in order.

Brigade is in early development.
